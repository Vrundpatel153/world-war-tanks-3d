SECTOR ALPHA · TACTICAL MAP INTEL

OPERATIONAL THEATER: Sector Alpha is a vast 4 square kilometer open-world combat testing ground featuring diverse topography, elevated bridges, asphalt roads, and industrial infrastructure.

KEY TOPOGRAPHICAL FEATURES:
  • Asphalt Highway Network: High-speed roadways connecting industrial hubs with concrete safety barriers and curbs.
  • Off-Road Plains & Embankments: Variable grassy terrain testing track suspension and hill-climbing torque.
  • Industrial Compound: Shelters, storage bins, conduits, and heavy steel drainage pipes.
  • Tactical Obstacles: Abandoned dump trucks, wooden pallets/logs, fire hydrants, and highway fences.

MISSION OBJECTIVE: Free-roam testing. Explore terrain, test vehicle maneuverability, practice high-velocity cannon ballistics, and engage autonomous combat targets across the sector.
